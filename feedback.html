Review Tips

  • Be Specific: Mention the page and paragraph or element where edits are requested.
  • Exact Replacements: When correcting text, paste the current text and the exact new text.

  • Page Dropdown: If your feedback relates to multiple sections or the site in general, you can leave the dropdown empty.
